Emotional coaching

Reconnect with yourself. Get your confidence back.

There are seasons in life when we need a space to listen to ourselves, reflect and find clarity. Emotional coaching is support focused on self-knowledge and personal growth.

My purpose isn't to tell you how to live your life, but to walk with you as you find your own answers and make decisions aligned with your values.

What can we work on together?

  • Building your self-esteem and confidence.
  • Recognizing your emotions and having tools to handle them day to day.
  • Recognizing your needs and setting healthy boundaries.
  • Working on your personal goals and motivation.
  • Creating habits that support your wellbeing.
  • Reconnecting with your strengths and values.

What are sessions like?

We start with a conversation to understand your goals. Then we work with questions, reflection exercises and practical actions for your week. Sessions are in person in Salt Lake City or online by video.
