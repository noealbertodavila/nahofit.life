Frequently asked questions

Is this therapy?

No. It's emotional coaching: it helps you build habits, manage stress and feel better. It doesn't diagnose or treat mental health conditions such as depression, anxiety or eating disorders. If you have or think you have one, we recommend a licensed therapist, and we can help you find one.

Do I need any coaching experience?

No. You can start right where you are. What matters is wanting to get to know yourself and work on your goals.

Do I have to work out to get coaching?

No. Emotional coaching and personal training can be taken separately, or together in the NAHOFIT Method.

Are sessions in person or online?

Both. You can have in-person sessions in Salt Lake City or online by video, wherever you are. Sessions are one-on-one.

What language are sessions in?

Spanish or English, whichever feels more comfortable.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the program and the format. Message me and in our first conversation I'll walk you through the options that fit you best.

What if I'm in a crisis?

If you're thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7) or 911 if you're in danger. Sessions are not an emergency service.
